Who Uses GeneVault & Clinical Value

1. Who is the User? (Doctors & Pharmacists)

Patients do not prescribe medications themselves. Cardiologists, oncologists, hospital pharmacists, and EHR copilot agents use this workstation to ensure prescribed medications match patient metabolic genetics before discharge.

2. Why AI Clouds Cannot Have Patient DNA

Hospitals cannot send a patient's unencrypted genomic file to external AI cloud APIs (like OpenAI or Google Cloud) because HIPAA and genetic privacy laws (GINA) strictly prohibit leaking permanent genetic identities.

3. Why the 3D Molecular Model is Essential

Doctors need to explain to patients (and visually verify themselves) why a medication is contraindicated. The 3D model shows the exact catalytic binding pocket affected by the patient's DNA mutation (e.g. CYP2C19*2 c.681G>A), showing visually why the drug cannot bind.
